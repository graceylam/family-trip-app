# Zora Highlight soundtracks

These seven recordings are original arrangements created for Zora and rendered from the app's own note sequences.

Instrument samples were rendered from **GeneralUser GS 2.0** by S. Christian Collins. The GeneralUser GS licence permits unrestricted use of the sound bank for music creation. Source and current documentation: https://github.com/mrbumpy409/GeneralUser-GS

Rendering used **TinySoundFont** by Bernhard Schelling, based on SFZero by Steve Folta, under the MIT License: https://github.com/schellingb/TinySoundFont

The GeneralUser GS SoundFont and TinySoundFont renderer are build-time tools and are not included in the app bundle. Only the finished Zora recordings are shipped.
